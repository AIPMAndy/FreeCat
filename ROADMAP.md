# FreeCat: world-class product roadmap

Mission: help anyone enjoy, understand and care for cats responsibly, even if they cannot or do not want to own one.

## Product loop

Pet a cat (first value in seconds) → discover a breed or a coat → identify visual clues → save a cat → return to a personal journal and learn → share the experience. No account is necessary for first value. Real care and virtual play remain clearly separate.

## Prioritized releases

P0: publish the static app; fix real Safari and Android issues; measure load time, successful touch interactions, anonymous voluntary feedback and accessibility; review veterinary content; add import/restore, shareable cat cards and stable breed URLs. No telemetry until a privacy-conscious measurement policy is implemented.

P1: improve procedural fur and reactions with profiling and device-quality fallback; develop an original touch-responsive 3D cat if it beats 2D on mobile performance; add better audio and optional haptics; licensed photo atlas and visually clear coat encyclopedia; research real users' most frequent care questions. Include language community contributions and RTL testing.

P2: offline/on-device photo recognition with a licensed training/evaluation dataset and explicit unknown/mixed-breed results. Evaluate across coats, lighting, age and geographic datasets; show limitations rather than false probability or pedigree claims. Veterinary expert review and region-aware care guidance. Journal import/backup, optional privacy-preserving sync.

P3: opt-in community cat cards and contributions with moderation, consent, reporting, no location exposure by default, no exploitative breeding promotion; rescue resource directory only with verified local partners; open SDK for cat mini-games and educational content.

## Acceptance and metrics (targets, not observed results)

- First useful interaction: under 5 seconds on representative phones and networks; purr and pet feedback actually work.
- Mobile performance: p75 LCP under 2.5 s and INP under 200 ms after public measurement; test low-end Android and iOS Safari.
- Accessibility: keyboard navigation, reduced motion, contrast, screen reader labels, no forced audio; assess WCAG 2.2 AA before claiming compliance.
- Growth: measure first successful pet, meaningful second action, 7-day return and shares; establish a baseline before setting growth commitments. Avoid gamifying time at the cost of welfare or attention.
- Operating costs: static free-tier hosting, zero required AI/API calls; revisit actual host limits and optional services as traffic grows.
- Safety: no false medical or pedigree claims; no default photo uploads or public personal data; no required purchases. Product quality is established by tests and real-world feedback, not by declaring it the best.
