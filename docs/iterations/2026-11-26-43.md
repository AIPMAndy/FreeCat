## Technical & design breakthroughs

1. **WebGL tactile fur displacement shader (with seamless Canvas 2D fallback)**:
   - Evaluated Canvas 2D circular stamping vs. single-pass WebGL fragment shader. Chose WebGL (`preserveDrawingBuffer: true`, premultiplied alpha output to eliminate semi-transparent fur edge halos on dark backgrounds) with automatic Canvas 2D fallback.
   - Multi-node spring-damped continuous trail (`uTrailPos[10]`, `uTrailVel[10]`): directional fur combing + subtle perpendicular fur parting + whisper-soft velvet sheen/compression modulation strictly inside per-pixel `bodyMask * (1.0 - faceMask)`.
   - Exact anatomical region ellipses (`head`, `back`, `flank`, `paws`, `tail`, `ear`) guarantee **0 pixel movement** on stationary sofa/blanket/linen backgrounds and **0 core distortion** on closed eyes/nose.
   - Living masked torso breathing (~4.4s asymmetric cycle, deepens gently during petting) and rare ear micro-twitch — strictly disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and on uploaded user photos (`raf === 0` at rest).
2. **Real purr audio linked to petting**:
   - Retains verified iOS/WebKit gesture-unlock rules (`purr-activation.test.cjs` 11/11, `purr-level.test.cjs` 11/11), zero pre-gesture fetch, local `assets/ariadne-purr.mp3` crossfade over labeled synth fallback, background-tap rejection, and smooth stroke-energy gain modulation.
3. **Art-grade visual redesign (desktop & mobile separately crafted)**:
   - Removed the bloated top hero stack and dead vertical whitespace; promoted a warm sunlit gallery frame (Daylight `#f5f0e8` / Night `#191e1b`) with exact non-distorting aspect-ratio fitting (`1.000` ratio on Milo across desktop & mobile).
   - Added benchmark-inspired circular cat thumbnail swatches right alongside the Purr toggle, `Another cat`, and `More` disclosure, fitting completely within 1440×900 and 390×844 viewports with zero overflow at 320px.
