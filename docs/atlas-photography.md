The image URLs are recorded in the atlasPhotos map in index.html. At present they are hosted by Wikimedia, not mirrored in this repository, because the execution environment cannot download their binaries. Links are lazy loaded only in the atlas with referrerpolicy=no-referrer. The remaining Bombay entry deliberately displays a missing-photo state. Several available candidates are explicitly classified as mixed/non-pedigree or domestic shorthair despite their filenames; none were substituted. Future local mirrors should preserve attribution, individual licensing and original provenance, document resizing, and verify actual image bytes. No AI-generated image is presented as a breed photograph.

Round 19 checked all eleven new source descriptions, photographer credits, licenses and displayed image pixels on 2026-11-25. Some examples are kittens or partial views (identified above); additional adult/full-body photographs are desirable. The Devon Rex file is a previously adjusted version with its original linked by Commons; FreeCat did not further edit it. Source-provided thumbnail resizing only, no newly generated content. Sources describe breed examples, not independent pedigree evidence.
